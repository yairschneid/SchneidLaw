Dedicated Legal Advocates Fighting for You

Schneid Marzin & Co. law firm deals with civil matters, including representing companies and private clients in various fields.

Our firm specializes in real estate and land transactions, taxes, including real estate taxation, international taxation and various exemptions from income tax, as well as in tort law, medical malpractice and the exercise of rights in the National Insurance Institute and against insurance companies. In addition, the firm provides its clients with services in drafting wills, inheritances and durable powers of attorney.
